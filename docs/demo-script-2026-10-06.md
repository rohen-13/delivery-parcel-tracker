# October 6 group demo

## Spoken introduction

We chose a Delivery Parcel Tracker for our IoT Monitoring System. It simulates a tracker attached to a parcel and displays delivery progress along a fictional route. Our first version is written in C and Raylib. Physical hardware is not connected.

## Demonstration sequence

1. Show PKG-001 at the depot with 0% progress.
2. Click Start and show the parcel moving through two checkpoints.
3. Click Pause and explain that delivery progress stops.
4. Click Resume and let the parcel reach the destination.
5. Show Delivered and 100% progress.
6. Click Reset and show the parcel back at the depot.
