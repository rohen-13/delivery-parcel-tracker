## Technical review

The tracker stores elapsed time and a running flag. Start enables movement until delivery completes. Update advances time only while running and ignores non-positive time increments. Progress is elapsed time divided by the 20-second journey duration. Arrival clamps elapsed time to 20 seconds and stops movement. Pause stops movement without clearing progress, so Start resumes the journey. Reset clears elapsed time and the running flag. The dashboard consumes progress to position the parcel and calls the tracking functions from its buttons.

Temperature, impact events, connection state and history are planned features. They are not present in today's retrieved prototype.
